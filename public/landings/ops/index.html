What the 8 AM brief contains

Read in this order. It is built from the hub files, so the brief is only as honest as the backlog.

  1. Links. This page, Command Center, Goal HQ.
  2. State of the business. Pipeline health (did mls-export run), the two operations (Siesta with Kelli, Longboat with Anne) against their next milestone, and anything in STRATEGY.md that changed in the last week.
  3. Milestones. The dated commitments: CCS letters in mailboxes in September, Siesta launch, Goal HQ tier pace, the July 2027 Landings purchase.
  4. Open work. Every P1 from BACKLOG.md, then a count of P2 and P3 by spoke, then the three that have been open longest.
  5. Today's one thing. The single action that moves a listing appointment closer, chosen from the P1 list.
The brief never re-derives a market number. Where it quotes one, it comes from the stats contract or the Goal HQ constants, with the as-of date.
