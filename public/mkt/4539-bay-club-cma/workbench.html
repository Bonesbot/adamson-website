Your Residence

The subject as the client sees it. Facts come from data.json; anything you type here overrides them (and feeds the adjustment math). Untick a row to hide it from the client page; hidden rows grey out. Saved with Save.

Multipliers

Arithmetic adjustments. These live in data.json and apply to every comp. Saved with Save.

Adjustment Grid

Blue = arithmetic, computed, do not touch. Bronze = your judgment buckets, keyed to the comp's MLS #. Sign convention: (+) subject superior to comp, (−) subject inferior. Green row = graded. Relevancy: A strongest comps · B supporting · C context · Exclude removes from the analysis. Setting relevancy marks the comp reviewed; the grid re-orders A, B, C, ungraded, excluded on load and after each save.
Client summary: prints under the comparables table on the client page (blank = nothing shown)

Notes

Internal note is private to this workbench. Client note prints on the shared CMA page under that comp.

Client Page: Sections, Titles & Fields

What the homeowner sees. Drag the handle to reorder sections top to bottom; untick a section to drop it from the client page and the PDF; retitle any section; untick a field to hide that column or fact. Hidden items turn grey here so you can see what you hid. Saved with Save.
Comparable Sales: columns shown
Market Update: columns shown

Market Update

Active competition and recent activity around the subject. Import a Stellar MLS CSV export (any saved search); rows sort by price, low to high. The note prints to the LEFT of each row on the client page. Bold and highlight print too. Everything here saves with Save, no rebuild.
Client page & PDF sections: forecast: not loaded
Forecast summary: prints under the Zillow section (blank = nothing shown)
Client summary: prints under the Market Update table (blank = nothing shown)

Cover Page

Full-page cover ahead of the report: your photo of the property, the address, your headshot and contact, the prepared-on date. Turn it on in the Sections card ("Cover page"). Photos are resized in the browser and stored with the CMA.

Online Estimates (AVMs)

Zestimate, Redfin Estimate, Realtor.com and the like for the subject. None of these sites offers a public API for third parties, so this is a look-up-and-type step: Open lookup pages opens each site's search for the subject address in new tabs; enter what you see. Shows on the client page when the section is ticked above.
