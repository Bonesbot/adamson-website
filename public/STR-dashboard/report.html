How to read this. Everything here is estimated from the inputs shown and last-12-month data on comparable short-term rentals.
Gross revenue — what guests pay for nights, before any costs.
Net operating income (NOI) — revenue minus operating costs, before the mortgage.
Cash flow — what is left after the mortgage payment too; the money that actually lands in (or leaves) your account.
Cash-on-cash return — yearly cash flow divided by the cash you put in (down payment, closing costs, furnishing).
Cap rate — NOI divided by price; the yield if you paid all cash.
Debt coverage (DSCR) — NOI divided by yearly mortgage payments; under 1.0 means income does not cover the loan.
ADR — average nightly rate actually earned. Occupancy — share of nights booked. RevPAR — revenue per available night (ADR × occupancy).
Low / Mid / High — the median, the average and the top quarter of comparable rentals, used as a range for what this property might do.
